A tiny escape from office

Darlooo 💜

I know today has been irritating. So for a few minutes, leave the office outside this page. No work. No politics. No fixing anything. Just you.

First… come here. 🫂

No advice. No “calm down.” No questions. You don't have to explain anything.

Come here, meri Darloo. 🫂

Just breathe for a moment. You can be irritated. You can be tired. You can have a horrible day and still be completely, wonderfully you. ❤️

Remember who you are. ✨

Whatever happened at work today is something that happened to you. It isn't a measurement of you.

Your worthisn't decided by someone's behaviour.
Your abilityisn't reduced by one frustrating day.
Your heartdoesn't need to become hard because someone was unfair.
And youdon't have to carry office home with you.

You once asked me… ❤️

“How do I complete you?”

I thought about that question properly.

You don't complete me because I'm incomplete without you. You complete the parts of my life that were always meant to be shared.

You're the person I naturally want to tell when something funny happens. The person whose opinion genuinely matters to me. The person who makes me look at myself a little more honestly.

You have made me more expressive, more thoughtful, and more aware of another person's feelings. You gave me a place where I could be completely myself.

And somehow, without trying, you became part of the way I experience my everyday life.

That's how you complete me.
Not by becoming my missing half —
by making the life I already have feel fuller because you're in it. ❤️

Things I love about you…

Not your face. Not your hair. Not your clothes. Just you.

The little thingsThe way you remember details that other people would forget.
Your careThe way you care about people and things that matter to you.
Your mindThe way you think, question, notice and form your own opinions.
Your realnessThe version of you that comes out when you aren't trying to impress anyone.

Emergency Mood Department 😂

Choose your current level of “I have had enough of people today.”

One thing before you go back to work…

Whatever happened today can stay at the office.

Don't let someone else's negativity rent space in your head for the rest of your day.

There is a whole life outside those four walls. And somewhere in that life, there's a stupid boy whose current mission is:

“Bas meri Darloo ka mood thoda sa theek ho jaaye.” 🥺❤️

No pressure. No fixing. Just a little love sent to your office. 💜
